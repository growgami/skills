# Competitor / Comparison Pages (Neobank / Fintech)

If `product-marketing.md` exists, read it first for positioning, approved claims, and competitors.

"[Your brand] vs [competitor]" and "[competitor] alternatives" queries are extremely high-intent — people searching them are choosing where to put their money. This is one of the best SEO plays in fintech, and one of the easiest to get wrong legally.

## Page types
- **Brand vs. brand** — "Acme vs Chime," head-to-head on fees, features, eligibility.
- **Alternatives** — "Chime alternatives," capturing demand around competitors.
- **Category best-of** — "best neobanks for [segment]" (overlaps with `programmatic-seo`).

## What makes them rank and convert
- A clear, scannable **comparison table** up top (fees, limits, features, regions) — also prime material for AI citation (`ai-seo`).
- An honest summary of who each option is best for. Pages that are transparently fair build more trust and convert better than obvious hit pieces.
- Real differentiation pulled from your positioning, not vague superlatives.
- Internal links to your account-opening and segment pages.

## Accuracy rules for financial comparisons (do not skip)
- **Accuracy is legally load-bearing.** Stating a competitor's fees, APY, or insurance status wrong isn't just bad SEO — it can be a false-advertising or disparagement problem. Cite the source and date for every competitor claim, and keep it current.
- **Use approved language** for your own rates/fees/insurance.
- **No misleading framing** — comparisons must be substantiable and sourced. Competitor-claim accuracy is the client's legal call, not this skill's; surface the sources and let them review.
- Respect competitor trademarks (comparative use is generally fine; avoid implying affiliation or using their logos in ways that suggest endorsement).

## Output
For each page: the comparison table fields, the "best for" framing per option, and the source/date for every factual competitor claim so the client can verify.
