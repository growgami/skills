# Seed Competitors — Neobanks & Fintechs (discovery fallback)

A grouped starting list of well-known neobanks/fintechs, used **only as a
fallback** when WebSearch discovery (Phase 1) is thin. Always prefer competitors
that genuinely overlap the target's **segment and region** — match by who the
product actually competes with, not just "another fintech."

This list is non-exhaustive and drifts over time (brands launch, merge, exit
markets). Confirm each candidate is live and comparable before using it, and add
newer entrants you find during discovery.
